Feed sounds

Like TikTok, every reel has its own sound. Scroll the phone and the music cuts straight to the next track. All ten are generated live in the browser with Web Audio, with no audio files. They're written from scratch in the style of each trend, not copied from real songs (the Bach is public domain). Tell me by number which ones to keep, change or drop.
